## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an intelligent, calm, and highly focused student learning environment. Inspired by the clarity of modern modular workspaces and the kinetic responsiveness of advanced AI assistants, the aesthetic bridges scholarly rigor with effortless digital clarity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for students, researchers, and lifelong learners navigating complex academic workloads. The UI cultivates deep concentration, removes cognitive clutter, and rewards progressive mastery. It feels warm, supportive, and structured—never gamified to the point of distraction, yet never sterile or dry.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Tactile Modern Workspace**: Crisp, structured paper-like surfaces layered over soft ambient backdrops.
- **Precision AI Integration**: Ambient intelligence represented not through heavy sci-fi neon or decorative glows, but through clear, micro-accented states, refined warm amber highlights, and sharp contextual summaries.
- **Editorial Legibility**: Deliberate typographic scale, spacious margins, and disciplined visual rhythm prioritizing reading comfort, active recall, and structured note-taking.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual hierarchy using deep slate tones anchored against luminous, low-strain paper whites, accented with energetic saffron and teal for algorithmic feedback and revision tracking.

### Primary Palette (Deep Navy Architecture)
- `primary`: `#0F172A` — Primary text, main navigational structures, active tabs, and solid high-intent interactions.
- `primary-container`: `#1E293B` — Secondary structural headers, active study sessions, and high-emphasis contextual cards.
- `primary-subtle`: `#334155` — Subordinate headings, active tool indicators, and medium-contrast structural dividers.

### Accent & Feedback Architecture
- `secondary` (Golden Amber): `#F59E0B` — Streaks, mastery alerts, AI-highlighted suggestions, and flashcard confidence markers.
- `secondary-deep`: `#D97706` — Hover states, high-contrast badges, and progress milestones.
- `tertiary` (Cognitive Teal): `#0D9488` — Active recall verification, successful retention tags, verified syllabus links, and revision metrics.
- `quaternary` (Saffron Flare): `#F97316` — High-priority deadlines, overdue spaced-repetition prompts, and urgent agenda markers.

### Canvas & Neutral Grounding
- `surface-canvas`: `#F8FAFC` — Base workspace background designed to lower eye fatigue during sustained study sessions.
- `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` — Focused content modules, note editors, and flashcard containers.
- `surface-muted`: `#F1F5F9` — Input backgrounds, gutter containers, and secondary sidebars.
- `text-primary`: `#0F172A` — Primary long-form reading and interface copy.
- `text-secondary`: `#475569` — Metadata, dates, secondary labels, and contextual helpers.
- `text-muted`: `#94A3B8` — Placeholders, disabled states, and keyboard shortcut indicators.
- `border-subtle`: `#E2E8F0` — Micro-borders, card seams, and grid dividers.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across headlines, interface controls, and body text. Its contemporary geometric curves and open counter forms deliver high readability at dense study scales, alongside a crisp, progressive tone.

### Scaling & Responsive Reflow
- Display and `headline-xl` styles step down automatically on screens smaller than 768px (`*-mobile` variants) to maintain viewport budget and prevent broken headlines in mobile flashcards or dashboard views.
- Long-form notes utilize `body-lg` capped at a reading length of 68-72 characters (`max-w-prose`) to sustain focus.
- Metadata, keyboard prompts, and AI confidence parameters use `label-md` and `code-sm` with tight tracking (+0.02em) for rapid visual scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8-point spatial cadence within a responsive 12-column grid system for desktop and a single/dual-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoint Behavior
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid, `gutter`: 1.5rem, `margin`: 2rem. Accommodates a 3-pane modular paradigm: Collapsible Navigator (Sidebar), Active Core Canvas (Editor/Flashcards), and Contextual AI Copilot Drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. The Copilot shifts to an overlay tray, and the core canvas auto-expands to full available width.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single column with edge-to-edge content container, `margin-mobile`: 1rem, `gutter-mobile`: 1rem. Bottom navigation bar anchors primary modes (Learn, Notes, AI, Progress).

### Whitespace Philosophy
Whitespace acts as a buffer against cognitive exhaustion. Cards and workspace zones maintain generous internal clearance (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to clearly separate modular ideas, while operational buttons, tag clusters, and table rows use tighter spatial units (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to preserve cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and artificial skeuomorphic layering in favor of **Tonal Surface Separation with Crisp Micro-Borders and Warm Ambient Diffusion**.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Flat Ground (Level 0)**: Background `#F8FAFC`. Completely non-elevated.
- **Card Surface (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and a subtle downward ambient tint: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Card Hover (Level 2)**: For clickable flashcards and active note blocks. Elevation lifts smoothly with `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border color shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (Level 3)**: Spaced repetition session trays, command palettes, and AI query overlays: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **AI Highlight Elevation**: Contextual hints and tutor popovers feature a faint warm golden boundary aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces refined rounded geometry with a base value of 16-18px for primary cards (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), creating a modern, approachable workspace that softens the rigors of heavy academic data.

### Corner Radius Implementation
- **Primary Content Cards & Panels**: 16px to 18px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to establish distinct, approachable workspace modules.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Dropdowns**: 10px to 12px (`rounded-md`) for ergonomic touch targets and unified visual rhythm.
- **Pills, Badges, and Status Chips**: Full pill radius (9999px) for clean distinction against rectangular editorial content.
- **Micro UI (Checkboxes, Toggles, Keycaps)**: 4px to 6px (`rounded-xs`) for structural clarity without feeling harsh.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) solid fill, white text (`#FFFFFF`), 10px corner radius, font weight 600. Hover: `#1E293B` with 1px subtle upward translation.
- **Secondary (AI / Accent)**: Warm Amber background (`#F59E0B`), text `#0F172A`, or an amber-tinted ghost variant (`#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text) for AI prompt generation and tutor triggers.
- **Outline / Minimal**: Ghost white with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), text `#334155`. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background with `#0F172A` text.
- **Icon Buttons**: 36x36px or 40x40px rounded squares (10px radius) with centered micro-icons (18px) for folding outlines and toggling focus mode.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface**: Background `#F8FAFC`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on active focus.
- **Borders**: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. On focus: 1.5px solid `#0F172A` accompanied by an outer micro-ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Typography**: `body-md`, `#0F172A` text, `#94A3B8` placeholder.
- **AI Command Bar (Omnibar)**: Centered floating input, 14px radius, featuring an amber-gold sparkle indicator (`#F59E0B`) on the trailing edge.

### Flashcards & Study Panels
- **Structure**: Pure white background `#FFFFFF`, 16px radius, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, ambient drop shadow level 1.
- **Flip / Reveal States**: Smooth 3D Y-axis transform (300ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)).
- **Rating Bar**: 4-part segmented control at the bottom of the card (Again: `#EF4444`, Hard: `#F97316`, Good: `#F59E0B`, Easy: `#0D9488`).

### Chips & Mastery Badges
- **Status Chips**: Pill-shaped (9999px), height 24px, padding 2px 10px, typography `label-sm`.
  - Retention Verified: Background `#CCFBF1`, text `#0F766E`.
  - Revision Scheduled: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
  - Concept Urgent: Background `#FFEDD5`, text `#C2410C`.
- **Subject Filter Chips**: Minimal outline, background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#475569`. Selected: background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border transparent.

### Lists & Note Trees
- **Row Architecture**: 40px height, inset border dividers (`#F1F5F9`), subtle rounded hover plate (8px radius, `#F1F5F9`).
- **Hierarchy Indicators**: Left-aligned 1.5px vertical guide lines in `#E2E8F0` representing nested notes and document outlines.
- **Completion Checkboxes**: 18x18px, 5px radius, 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Checked state: solid `#0D9488` with crisp white checkmark vector and strikethrough typography on adjacent label.

### AI Tutor Callout Block
- **Container**: Seamless inset banner within long-form notes. Background: Linear gradient from `#FFFBEB` to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Accent**: 3px solid left accent strip in `#F59E0B`.
- **Header**: Inline label with AI icon, typography `label-md` in `#92400E`. Actionable suggested revisions displayed as interactive pill tags along the bottom.
